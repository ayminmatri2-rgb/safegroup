Safe Group — three mark options

Same cyan (#09C4DC), same navy ground, three different marks

Option 1 — Refined frame
SAFE GROUP Risk & Assurance

Closest to the current mark, just tightened up — a single hairline frame instead of a double border, letterforms centred and slightly larger, and one clean corner tick instead of a heavier bracket. Reads as a refinement, not a change, if you want continuity.

Option 2 — Seal / monogram
SAFE GROUP Risk & Assurance

A seal/badge form — a double ring around the initials, closer to how an assurance or advisory firm marks itself (think notary seal, not tech logo). Leans into the "assurance" half of the new positioning; works well small on a business card or letterhead.

Option 3 — Node mark
SAFE GROUP Risk & Assurance

Borrows the pulsing node/geo-marker motif already used across the site (hero backdrop, live status dots) and puts it directly in the mark — a small tracked point above the initials. Ties the logo itself to the "real-time, on-the-ground" story rather than being a generic badge.
