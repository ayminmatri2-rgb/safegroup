Watchpoint is our technology partner.

Whether you're already working with another provider or running an in-house risk team, Watchpoint gives you real-time tracking, verified attendance, and an auditable incident record over your existing field operation — with or without an SG services contract.

Capabilities

Built for the field. Trusted in the boardroom.

Every feature exists because Safe Group's own field operations needed it first.

Real-time personnel tracking

A live map of every officer, patrol, and site — positions, status, and movement history in one operational picture.

AI incident classification

Incoming field reports are classified and prioritised automatically, so critical incidents surface first — not after the shift report.

Offline-first mobile app

Built for low-connectivity environments. Officers report and check in with no signal; everything syncs automatically when coverage returns.

Geofenced clock-in

Attendance verified by location. Officers can only clock in inside their assigned geofence — no exceptions, no disputes.

Digital reports & audit trail

Every patrol, occurrence, and escalation is logged, timestamped, and exportable — ready for client audit at any moment.

Client command dashboard

A live window into your risk operation — KPIs, incidents, and compliance, without waiting for the monthly report.

Deployment

Live on your sites in three steps.

01

Deploy & geofence your sites

We map your sites, posts, and patrol routes into Watchpoint and draw the geofences that govern clock-in and patrol verification.

02

Your workforce reports from the field

Officers clock in, log occurrences, and complete patrols from the offline-first mobile app — with or without connectivity.

03

You monitor and audit in real time

AI classifies incoming incidents, dashboards show compliance live, and every event is exportable for audit.
