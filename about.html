About Safe Group

Libyan roots. Built for complex risk.

Safe Group (SG) is one of the earliest private safety and security risk firms established in Libya — founded in Tripoli in 2009, where it remains headquartered today.

From a Tripoli guarding practice to a risk consultancy.

SG was founded in Tripoli at a time when professional field risk management barely existed in Libya. From the beginning, the company set itself apart from generic guarding outfits: supervised posts, documented procedures, and reporting that clients could actually audit.

As our clients' needs grew beyond the gate, so did we — adding mobile risk response, structured risk assessment, and financial due diligence for organisations making high-stakes decisions in a complex market.

Watchpoint, our technology partner, reflects the same discipline: real-time visibility and an auditable record for any organisation that manages field personnel — whether or not SG provides them.
